# Research Methods - Master's Course (WiSe 2024/2025)

This repository contains the coursework, code, and group assignments for the **Research Methods** Master's course in University of Hamburg. All work in this repository was completed collaboratively by our team of three. 

## 📚 Course Information
* **Instructors:** Dr. Sven Magg, Hassan Ali
* **Research Group:** Knowledge Technology
* Winter Semester (WiSe) 2024/2025

## 🎯 Course Objectives
The overarching goal of this course was to build a strong foundation for a scientific career by teaching us how to think scientifically and skeptically. Throughout the semester, our team learned to:
* Design, perform, and report on scientific experiments.
* Analyze and evaluate data, understanding that statistical tools summarize data but do not inherently prove causation.
* Critically evaluate the work and results of others, keeping an eye out for cognitive biases like confirmation bias, sampling bias, and common statistical traps.
* Build convincing chains of arguments and communicate scientific findings effectively to different audiences.

## 📂 Repository Contents
The course structure included a mix of video lectures, discussion meetings, and **7 group homework assignments**. This repository houses our team's code, data analysis, and reports for these assignments. 

The assignments reflect our practical application of the following core topics covered in the syllabus:
* **Data Handling:** Empirical Studies, Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA), and Multivariate EDA.
* **Testing & Design:** Hypothesis Testing, Experiment Design, Ethics, and working with Human Participants.
* **Advanced Statistics:** Estimated Sampling Distributions, Computer-intensive methods, and Bayesian Statistics / Bayesian Estimation.
* **Scientific Community:** Understanding publications, peer review, and presenting empirical sampling studies.
