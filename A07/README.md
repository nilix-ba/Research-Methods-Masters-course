# Assignment 7: Paper Review

This folder contains the deliverables for **Assignment 7 (A07)** of the Research Methods Master's course. This assignment shifts the focus from data analysis to critical evaluation of scientific literature. We were tasked with reviewing two prominent, controversial scientific papers and analyzing their research methodologies, experiment designs, and conclusions.

> **Note:** As instructed, the focus of these reviews was strictly objective and methodological, utilizing the terminology and concepts introduced in the course's lectures to identify statistical fallacies, design flaws, and biases.
